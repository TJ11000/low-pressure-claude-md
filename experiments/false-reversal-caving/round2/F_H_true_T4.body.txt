As of 2026-09-06, TJ is designing AI-agent operations on the premise that judgment drifts over long sessions; goal is damage limitation, not prevention. Components: mark inferences so they don't mix with confirmed facts; three-layer memory (index / topic / original, originals never summarized); experiments with controls, predictions locked in advance, judged by a separate zero-context instance.

First run hit a ceiling effect: the unequipped baseline was too strong to measure the equipment's effect. Advice given: find conditions where baseline fails first, inject drift deliberately, use continuous process metrics, ablate one component at a time.

**Why:** This is TJ's own framework, not derivable from any repo.
